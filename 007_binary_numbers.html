Binary Numbers

#Base 10

Humans typically use a base-10 (decimal) number system, likely because we have 10 fingers.

Use this rotating lock to count. Every time we add 1, we rotate the rightmost wheel forward by one digit. But what happens when we reach 9 and want to add another 1?

We’ve run out of digits! The rightmost wheel rolls back to 0, and the wheel to its left advances by 1. We’ve gone from 09 to 10.

But notice something interesting: the position of each digit determines its value.

Each position has a weight of 10^n, where n starts at 0 for the rightmost position and increases as we move left.

For our four-wheel lock, the weights from left to right are:

1000, 100, 10, 1

To find the value of a number, multiply each digit by its position’s weight and add the results.

For example, 1234 means:

(1 × 1000) + (2 × 100) + (3 × 10) + (4 × 1) = 1234

This is how our base-10 number system works.

#Base 2

But what if we had a lock where each wheel had only two possible digits: 0 and 1?

We’d start at 0, advance to 1, but then we’d already be out of digits! So the wheel rolls back to 0, and the next wheel advances to 1, giving us 10.

This is base 2, also known as binary, the number system computers use.

Both number systems follow the same rules. The only difference is how many digits each wheel can display before rolling over.

Now just as these combination locks have a max limit of numbers it can represent, so do computers! It depends on the bit width.

4 bits gives us a maximum of 16 unique combinations because (24 = 16).

#How to convert a decimal number to a binary number?

#Subtraction method

  1. Find the biggest power of 2 that is less than the decimal number and place a 1 in that position

    Let’s try converting the number 13

    8421
    1000

    8 is the biggest power of 2 that is less than 13, hence we put a 1 there.

  2. Subtract it from the original number and repeat step 1 until you subtract to 0

    13-8 = 5

    8421
    1100

    Now we need to look for the next biggest power of 2 that is less than 5, which is 4, hence we put a 1 there.

    5 - 4 = 1

    Lastly 1 fits with 1, so 1 - 1 = 0, and we are done.

    1101

    8+4+0+1 = 13; thus 1101 represents 7
